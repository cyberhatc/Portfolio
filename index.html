The Origin Story

Started writing code at age 14 — not because a teacher told me to, but because I wanted to build things that didn't exist yet. That obsession hasn't slowed down.

I'm a Full-Stack & Mobile App Developer who thrives at the intersection of cross-platform engineering, 3D interactive simulations, and algorithmic problem-solving. My tools of choice: Flutter, FastAPI, Firebase, Unity, and Blender.

5-time hackathon runner-up — each loss taught me more than any win could. I build systems, not just apps. From satellite tracking to virtual chemistry labs, from OS-level retail management to AI assistants, I don't pick easy problems.

Currently architecting CyberHATC — my personal dev brand and the umbrella for everything I ship.
